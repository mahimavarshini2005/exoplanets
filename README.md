# Exoplanets
NASA has discovered more than 6000 exoplanets out of the billions that exists. There are more planets than stars in the galaxy. The exoplanets discovered are situated in a small region of our Milky way Galaxy located light years away from Earth. In this repository, I will identify exoplanets using publicly available NASA telescope data from telescopes Kepler and TESS.
Database of the planets discovered from TESS: https://exo.mast.stsci.edu/
TESS stars data: https://archive.stsci.edu/missions-and-data/tess
Catalogue of stars from telescopes: https://mast.stsci.edu/portal/Mashup/Clients/Mast/Portal.html
